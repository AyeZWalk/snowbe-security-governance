# SnowBe Security Governance Case Study

In Project & Portfolio V: Cybersecurity, my team assessed security practices at a fictional online retailer and proposed improvements to how it manages risk. I prepared presentations on the team's maturity assessment and how threat modeling connects policy decisions to system and software development.

## How we assessed maturity

We reviewed the case evidence for three things: whether a control existed, whether the process was documented, and whether staff could repeat it. Account provisioning had clearer evidence of a repeatable process. Backup, incident response, and patch management needed stronger evidence of ownership, testing, and review.

## Decisions I presented

| Area | Decision and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Access control | Define who grants and reviews access. Review privileged accounts first because they can reach sensitive systems and data. |
| Incident readiness | Assign an owner and define escalation steps so staff can respond consistently when an issue is reported. |
| Patch management | Track each update from prioritization through validation and document exceptions with an owner and review date. |
| System and software life cycles | Review threats before design and release decisions, then revisit those threats after major changes. |

## My contribution

I prepared the Week 3 maturity and Week 4 threat-modeling presentations and explained the rationale behind the team's recommendations in both decks. The underlying assessment, security plan, and policy drafts were team deliverables.

The course used a five-tier maturity rubric and historical CMMC v1.02 materials. The recommendations describe an academic planning exercise. Implementation and control effectiveness would require separate evidence.
